What the Pharmacy Health Score measures

The score is designed as an early-warning and prioritisation tool. It combines financial indicators with pharmacy operating controls.

Profitability · 20%Gross margin and estimated operating profitability.
Inventory · 25%Turnover, expiry exposure, dead stock, stock-outs and count discipline.
Working Capital · 15%Cash tied up in inventory relative to business throughput.
Operations · 15%SOPs, reconciliation and day-to-day process control.
Compliance · 15%Licence tracking and controlled expiry / return processes.
People & Systems · 10%Training, role clarity and management reporting.
