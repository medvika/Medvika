Registered Pharmacist

Bharatpur, Rajasthan · On-site · Full-time

About the role

Registered Pharmacist for a client pharmacy in Bharatpur, Rajasthan. Dispense medicines in accordance with pharmacy regulations, validate prescriptions, counsel patients, handle billing and pharmacy records, monitor inventory and expiry, and support daily pharmacy operations. D.Pharm or B.Pharm and valid State Pharmacy Council registration required.

Key responsibilities

  • Dispense medicines and validate prescriptions in accordance with applicable regulations.
  • Support billing, patient counselling, inventory control, and expiry monitoring.
  • Maintain pharmacy records and coordinate with the store team.

Eligibility

D.Pharm / B.Pharm with valid State Pharmacy Council registration

Salary: As per experience and qualifications.
