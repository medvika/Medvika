Registered Pharmacist

Pharmacy Operations · Pan India / Project Locations · Full-time / Contract / Project Based
Open

Support retail and hospital pharmacy operations, dispensing, compliance, stock control, patient counselling, audits and pharmacy improvement projects for Medvika and its clients.

  • Dispense medicines in accordance with applicable pharmacy regulations.
  • Validate prescriptions and provide appropriate patient counselling.
  • Monitor inventory, expiry, FEFO/FIFO and controlled medicine records.
  • Support pharmacy setup, SOP implementation and operational improvement.
  • Assist with physical stock audits, reconciliation and discrepancy reporting.
  • Maintain statutory pharmacy records and support regulatory compliance.
  • Coordinate with doctors, suppliers and healthcare teams when required.

Preferred: D.Pharm / B.Pharm / M.Pharm with valid State Pharmacy Council registration. Pharmacy software knowledge and willingness to travel are an advantage.

Pharmacy Operations Manager

Operations · North India / Project Locations · Full-time / Project Based
Open

Lead and support retail pharmacy operations, SOP implementation, inventory control, staff productivity, compliance and store performance improvement.

  • Review pharmacy operations and identify process gaps.
  • Improve inventory, expiry, purchase, sales and stock-control practices.
  • Implement SOPs, audit controls and operational reporting.
  • Train store teams and support new pharmacy launches.
  • Track KPIs, profitability and execution of corrective actions.

Pharmacy Audit Associate / Auditor

Audit · Delhi NCR / North India · Project Based
Open

Support physical stock audits, batch and expiry verification, reconciliation and reporting assignments for pharmacies and healthcare clients.

  • Conduct physical stock counting and verification.
  • Check batch, expiry and quantity variances.
  • Follow audit instructions and maintain accurate count sheets.
  • Escalate discrepancies to the audit lead.
  • Support night-shift or time-bound audit assignments when required.
