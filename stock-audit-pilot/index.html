What the portal does

From system stock to final audit report

The pilot is built around a practical pharmacy stock-verification workflow rather than a generic spreadsheet or simple counting form.

Current Stock Import

Upload current stock from Excel or CSV and map item, batch, pack, quantity, rate and expiry data.

Smart Quantity Handling

Interpret pharmacy pack-and-loose quantities and keep reconciliation based on normalized stock units.

Teams & Zones

Create controlled counting zones and teams before allocating stock for physical verification.

Physical Counting

Record physical stock, batch and expiry information while flagging recount and condition issues.

Reconciliation

Compare system and physical stock to identify matched, excess, short and missing inventory.

Expiry & Damage Review

Surface expired, near-expiry and damaged stock findings for management attention.

Financial Variance

Review shortage, excess and inventory exposure using purchase-rate based valuation where available.

Professional PDF Report

Generate a structured Medvika audit report with reconciliation, risk, expiry and completion findings.

How it works

A controlled six-step audit workflow

Each stage feeds the next so the final reconciliation is built from the same audit dataset.

1

Create Audit

Set up the pharmacy audit project and basic audit requirements.

2

Import Stock

Upload system/current stock and validate quantity interpretation.

3

Allocate Teams

Define zones, create counting teams and allocate stock lines.

4

Count Physically

Enter or import physical counts with batch, expiry and condition details.

5

Reconcile

Review system versus physical quantity and material variances.

6

Generate Report

Produce a professional audit report for management review.
