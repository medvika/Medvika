Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the UP pharmacy website rule definitely effective 1 November 2026?

News outlets and regulatory-update services cite that date as expected implementation. The trade association has sought a change. Confirm the original signed circular and any updates with UPFSDA or your district drug inspector.

Do I need to replace my billing software?

No. Medvika can assess your available CSV/Excel export and develop a suitable upload process. Real-time PMS integration is a separate technical option.

Will your website automatically update my stock?

Weekly stock publication may be manual or assisted. Automatic synchronization is only possible where suitable software access and integration are available and authorised.

Are medicine purchase prices public for every retail medical store?

Media reporting distinguishes the additional price-disclosure obligations of hospital-attached pharmacies. The exact scope should be confirmed against the official order before publishing commercial purchase rates.

Is this a government-approved compliance certificate?

No. Medvika provides technology and implementation assistance. Regulatory compliance is subject to the official requirements and the pharmacy's accurate information and ongoing updates.
