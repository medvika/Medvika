Common Questions

Pharmacy Startup FAQs

How much investment is required to start a pharmacy?

Investment depends on location, store size, rent, interiors, licensing, software, initial inventory and staffing. Medvika can help you prepare a practical cost plan based on your proposed pharmacy.

What licences are generally required?

Requirements vary by pharmacy type and location. Applicants should confirm current drug licence, registration, tax and local requirements with the appropriate authorities and qualified professionals.

How long does a pharmacy setup usually take?

The timeline depends on licensing, premises readiness, store interiors, inventory planning, recruitment and software implementation. A structured plan can reduce avoidable delays.

Can Medvika assist with inventory and SOP planning?

Yes. Medvika provides guidance for initial inventory planning, procurement workflows, expiry management, billing, returns, stock control and standard operating procedures.

Do you provide support after the pharmacy opens?

Medvika can support operational reviews, staff training, inventory optimisation, pharmacy audits and business improvement after launch.
